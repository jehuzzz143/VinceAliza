Background music placeholder
=============================

Add your background music track here as:

  wedding-music.mp3

This is referenced by the <audio> element in index.html
(id="bg-audio") and played automatically once the guest taps
open the invitation card.

Suggested sources for royalty-free music:
- https://pixabay.com/music/search/wedding/
- https://freemusicarchive.org/
- https://www.bensound.com/

Keep the file reasonably small (under ~5MB, MP3 format) so it
starts playing quickly on mobile connections.
